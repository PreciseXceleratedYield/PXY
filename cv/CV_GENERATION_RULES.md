# MASTER CV GENERATION RULES — INTERNAL ONLY

**Authoritative source:** Always read this GitHub master before producing a master or job-tailored CV.

**Four-page target and content hierarchy:** Page 1 contains the executive profile and value proposition; for tailored CVs, title the value proposition **WHY I AM A FIT FOR THIS ROLE**. Start **WORK EXPERIENCE** on Page 2. Within WORK EXPERIENCE, retain employment history with technical and business leadership capabilities integrated into the relevant SAP Asia career-stage paragraphs, followed by **EXECUTIVE ACCOMPLISHMENTS** as a subordinate section. Do not restore standalone keyword-inventory sections unless explicitly requested. **CERTIFICATIONS** and **EDUCATION** are independent main sections following the entire WORK EXPERIENCE group. Flow continuously from Page 2 onward without forced breaks or large blank areas; target four readable ATS-friendly pages and check the last-page fill, but do not sacrifice readability or invent experience merely to fill space.

**ATS / work history:** SAP Asia Pte Ltd is ONE employer, ONE consolidated role, ONE employment date range. Mention internal roles within descriptive paragraphs only; no internal dates or separate employer entries. Microsoft India and Microsoft Singapore contract remain separate records. Preserve truthful distinctions between hands-on delivery, program governance, advisory exposure, and ecosystem familiarity; keyword coverage is not a claim of hands-on proficiency with every named product.

**Keyword integrity:** Retain ATS-relevant technology and leadership terms in natural work-experience context. Avoid long transaction-code inventories, duplicate skill lists, and unsupported claims of hands-on experience with emerging AI products; include current AI/automation projects only where independently evidenced.

**Change control:** Tailor Page 1 only for job-specific applications unless the user explicitly requests changes to other sections. Do not silently modify the approved master experience or skills; verify exported PDF page count and readable text.

**Source separation:** The rules in this file are for CV generation only. Never print or export them as part of the CV. The resume content lives in `cv/SAPAIBHANUMASTER.md`. Keep meta-instructions and editorial disclaimers out of the visible resume.
